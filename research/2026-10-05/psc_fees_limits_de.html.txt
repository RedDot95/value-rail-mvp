 All fees and limits at a glance 
 PaysafeCard fees 
Both the use of PaysafeCard and the payment are generally free of charge. However, the following fees may arise (you can read more detailed information in our 
GTC
):
Monthly fee:
 the monthly fee of 4 EUR will be charged for the first time 30 (thirty) days after purchase.
 Don't want to pay a Service fee? 
Sign up
 for your PaysafeCard account today and redeem the voucher. That is all it takes.
Conversion fee:
 PaysafeCard charges a fee for transactions with currency conversions. The PaysafeCard 
currency calculator
 immediately gives you the current exchange rate.
Refund fee:
 5% (max. EUR 5) per refund will be deducted from the amount to be paid out.
 Benefit from our Self-Service Offering to save fees. 
Create a Paysafecard Account
, upload your PIN, 
activate Account and Card
 and spend the balance with your new Paysafe Mastercard or use the funds via bank transfer.
PaysafeCard account service fee:
 From the 13th month, a monthly fee of 2 EUR will be deducted from the PaysafeCard account if the customer has not carried out any transactions in the PaysafeCard account within 12 months.
 Want to avoid a service fee? Simply 
sign up for PaysafeWallet
 and transfer your funds into the new wallet. You can then spend them anywhere with you new Debit Card and via bank transfer.
