A 1.75% fee applies to international payments in non-euro zones, and 2% in the rest of the world.

The reload fee for voucher redemption may vary between 0% and 6% depending on the transaction. Reloads via cryptocurrencies are limited to €20,000 per day.
4
The applicable fee for non-free reloads is 1.5%.
5
The full service fee charged by the ATM operator is passed on for national and eurozone ATMs. For non-euro and other international areas: €0.55 + 1% of the amount, minimum €0.75. ATM withdrawals outside of Spain are limited to a maximum of €450 per day.
6
Transfers to another entity that is not the same holder or Pecunpay are subject to a €1.50 fee.
