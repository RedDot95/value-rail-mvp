 WHAT YOU GET 
 One BuySellVouchers API for catalogue, wallet, purchases, and codes.
 Live catalogue.
 Browse live gift cards and digital products with region, denomination, list price, stock, seller ratings, and payment methods — filterable by category, seller, or product.
 Inner wallet.
 Read available and pending balances on your marketplace wallets. Purchase uses those wallets only — not card checkout.
 Programmatic purchase.
 Place an order with product, quantity, and wallet. The API accepts the purchase, then you poll until the order completes.
 Purchased codes.
 After completion, fetch your order items. Text codes and image URLs are returned to the buyer of that order only.
