How paying works
Your assistant never sees your card details or your gift card code. It prepares the order, then you pay and receive the code yourself.
Crypto, Binance Pay and bank transfer orders are paid on the payment page your assistant links to. Card payments, including Apple Pay and Google Pay where available, open on a secure CoinGate Gift Cards page, where you first confirm your email with a one-time code.

Questions about buying with AI
What is MCP?
The Model Context Protocol is an open standard that lets AI assistants use outside tools. With our server added, your assistant can search the CoinGate Gift Cards store and place orders for you, much like it searches the web.
Do I need an account?
No. There is no sign-up, login or API key. You give an email address for the order, and the code is sent there.
Which assistants does it work with?
Any app that supports remote MCP servers, including Claude, ChatGPT, Claude Code, Codex CLI, Gemini CLI, Cursor, VS Code, Windsurf and Zed. Apps that only run local servers can connect through mcp-remote.
Does it cost extra?
No. The server is free to use and prices are the same as on the website. Your assistant shows the full total, including any payment fee, before you order.
What does my assistant see?
The catalog, prices and the status of your orders. You enter payment details on the payment page, and gift card codes go only to your email, never to the assistant.
Why do I get an email code before paying by card?
It proves the email on the order is yours, so a gift card code can only go to the person who pays for it. The code comes from CoinGate Gift Cards and you enter it on the payment page.
Can my assistant buy without asking me?
It is told to show you the card, the total and the terms and to wait for your yes before ordering. Even then, nothing is charged until you pay at the link yourself.
